## FPS Deadline
Full Payment Submission (FPS) must be sent to HMRC on or before each payday.

## Apprenticeship Levy

- **Rate**: 0.5% of total pay bill
- **Annual allowance**: £15,000 (per group of connected companies)
- **Threshold**: Applies to employers with annual pay bill > £3,000,000
- **Reporting**: Via EPS (Employer Payment Summary), not FPS
- **Pay bill definition**: Sum of all employee earnings subject to Class 1 secondary NICs (i.e. total gross earnings before employee deductions)
- **Frequency**: Reported and paid each month via EPS
- **Connected companies**: Share one £15,000 allowance across the group

### Example
- Monthly pay bill: £500,000
- Monthly gross levy: £500,000 × 0.5% = £2,500
- Monthly allowance: £15,000 ÷ 12 = £1,250
- Monthly levy payable: £2,500 − £1,250 = **£1,250**
